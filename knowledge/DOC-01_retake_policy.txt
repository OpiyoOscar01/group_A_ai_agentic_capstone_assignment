DOC-ID: DOC-01 | Title: Academic Regulations – Retakes and Supplementary Examinations | Source: Team-synthesised from public Makerere handbook structure (2026) | Version: 1.0 2026-09-12
A student who fails a course with a mark below 50% may, on recommendation of the School Board, be permitted to retake the course when next offered. Only one retake attempt is permitted per failed course under normal progression. A supplementary examination may be granted where a student misses an examination for verified medical or emergency reasons, with evidence submitted to the Dean within 14 days. Retake registration must be completed through the student portal before the semester deadline. The retake fee, where applicable, is paid at the Bursar. No student may retake a passed course to improve a grade without written approval from the Head of Department. Appeals against retake decisions are lodged with the School Registrar.
