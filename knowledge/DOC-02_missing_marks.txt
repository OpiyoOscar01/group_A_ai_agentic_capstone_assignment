DOC-ID: DOC-02 | Title: Missing Marks Procedure | Source: Team-synthesised departmental guidance (2026) | Version: 1.0 2026-09-12
Where marks are missing on the student portal 30 days after results publication, the student must report to the Course Lecturer first, then file a support case with the Department Office if unresolved within 7 days. The case must state course code, semester, lecturer name and proof of coursework/exam attendance. The Department Office verifies with the lecturer and Examinations Office within 14 working days. The student can track case status using the case ID. Cases remain Pending until reviewed by staff. Students must not pay any unofficial fee to correct missing marks.
