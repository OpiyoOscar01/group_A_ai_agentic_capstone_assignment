DOC-ID: DOC-09 | Title: Examination Rules | Source: Team-synthesised (2026) | Version: 1.0
Students must present a valid student ID and examination permit. Mobile phones are prohibited in examination halls. Late arrival beyond 30 minutes is not permitted. Malpractice cases are referred to the Disciplinary Committee and cannot be handled by this agent. Illness during examinations must be reported to the invigilator and Dean with medical evidence within 48 hours.
