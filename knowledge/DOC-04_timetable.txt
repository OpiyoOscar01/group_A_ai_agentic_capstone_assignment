DOC-ID: DOC-04 | Title: Timetable and Class Attendance Policy | Source: Team-synthesised (2026) | Version: 1.0
Official class timetables are published by the School Timetabling Officer before semester start. Students check timetables using a valid 10-digit student ID. Attendance of at least 75% is required to sit examinations. Timetable clashes must be reported within week one. Sample record: BSE4104 Emerging Trends in Software Engineering, Monday 09:00-11:00, CIT Block B Room 3. Changes are announced on department notice boards.
