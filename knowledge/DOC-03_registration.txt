All undergraduate students must register courses on the student portal within the first three weeks of the semester. Late registration attracts a surcharge set by the Academic Registrar. A minimum of 15 credit units and maximum of 24 credit units per semester applies. Registration requires clearance of prior semester fees. Students with registration holds must visit the School Registrar with admission letter and fee receipts. Registration issues can be filed as a support case under category registration.
