DOC-ID: DOC-12 | Title: Data Handling and Privacy Note | Source: Team-created (2026) | Version: 1.1
The system uses only synthetic student, timetable and case records for prototyping. No real student records are stored or sent to external AI services. Retained memory is limited to one item: the active case ID (CASE-XXXX) of the signed-in student. It is kept for at most 30 days, or until the case is no longer open, and is used only to answer read-only follow-up questions such as case status. Students may request deletion of their session memory at any time by asking the system to forget their session. Staff access case queues only for review and cannot read session memory. No data is shared outside the approved application boundary without authorisation. Policy reference for privacy refusals: DOC-12.
